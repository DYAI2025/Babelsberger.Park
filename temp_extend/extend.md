## 🧘 Yoga & Achtsamkeit
**Concept:** "Resakralisierung" (temporarily transforming public space into a spiritual one).

### Key Spots
*   **Park Babelsberg:** Dynamic, hilly terrain. Favorite for "Bewegte Meditation".
*   **Neuer Garten (Heiliger See):** Flat, proximity to water. Pro-tip: **Wiese Ostufer** (coordinates: 52.4140, 13.0690) – quiet, morning sun.
*   **Neuer Garten (Marmorpalais):** Protected area with architectural view (coordinates: 52.4145, 13.0695).

### Rituals & Groups
*   **Elena's Group:** Regularly meet **Fridays 8:30 AM** at Entrance Alt Nowawes. Perform "Moving Meditation" regardless of weather.
*   **Individual Practice:** Focus on Lenné's sightlines as meditative anchors.

### Tips
*   **Equipment:** Robust mat (wet grass), "Zwiebellook" (dress in layers), screen for insects (summer).
*   **Best Times:** Summer 6-8 AM (cool/quiet).

**Concept:** "Guerilla-Kuratierung" (Bottom-up storytelling through digital clues).

### Featured Caches
*   **Park Babelsberg 01 (GC19KQZ):** Multi-cache, 8 stations, approx. 2-5 km. Highlights: Marstall, Flatowturm, Dampfmaschinenhaus.
*   **Park Babelsberg 02 (GC1A3B2):** Alternative Multi-cache highlighting Gerichtslaube and river views.
*   **Mystery Caches:** "Code orange" at Glienicker Brücke (Agent exchange theme).

### Rules
*   **UNESCO Status:** Strict "Wegegebot" (stay on paths). No night caches. No drones.
*   **Muggels:** High tourist density; discrete logging required.

## 🚻 Practical Information & Infrastructure

### Toilets (WCs)
*   **Data Source:** `wc.geojson`
*   **Standorte:** Schloss Babelsberg, Flatowturm, Dampfmaschinenhaus (Park Babelsberg); Marmorpalais, Cecilienhof (Neuer Garten).
*   **Cost:** Partially 0.50€. Accessibility (Wheelchair) varies.

### Parking
*   **Main Lot:** "Am Neuen Garten" (GPS: 52.419083, 13.071547). ~50 spots. Often full by 11 AM on weekends.
*   **P+R:** S-Bhf Griebnitzsee (Free). 15 min walk to Park Babelsberg.

### ÖPNV (Public Transport)
*   **Bus 694:** Best for direct access ("Park Babelsberg" stop).
*   **Bus 690:** "Rathaus Babelsberg" (800m walk).
*   **Tram 93:** "Lutherplatz" (1.2 km walk).

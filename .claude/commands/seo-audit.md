## Context

Static bilingual park website at `park.babelsberger.info`. All HTML pages live in `park-babelsberg/`. SEO gaps directly impact Google Discover eligibility and AdSense revenue.

## Your Task

Perform a comprehensive SEO audit across all pages and report gaps.

### Checks to Perform

1. **Open Graph Tags** — Every page must have:
   - `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, `og:image`, `og:type`, `og:locale`, `og:site_name`
   - Blog posts additionally: `article:published_time`, `article:modified_time`, `article:section`
   - Twitter Card: `twitter:card=summary_large_image`

2. **Schema.org JSON-LD** — Every page must have structured data:
   - Blog posts: `BlogPosting` schema
   - Attraction pages: `Place` or `TouristAttraction` schema
   - Hub/park pages: `ItemList` or `Place` schema

3. **Sitemap Coverage** — Read `sitemap.xml` and cross-check:
   - Every HTML page in `park-babelsberg/` should have a sitemap entry
   - Every `park-babelsberg/blog/*.html` must be listed
   - `<lastmod>` dates should not be stale (older than content changes)

4. **Canonical URLs** — Every page must have:
   - `<link rel="canonical" href="https://park.babelsberger.info/[path]/">`

5. **Meta Robots** — Blog posts must have:
   - `<meta name="robots" content="max-image-preview:large, max-snippet:-1, max-video-preview:-1">`

6. **Routing** — Check `vercel.json` has routes for all pages

7. **Translation Sync** — Check `de.json` and `en.json` have matching keys

8. **Internal Cross-Links**:
   - Blog posts should have "Den Park entdecken" CTA section
   - Key attraction pages should have "Aus unserem Magazin" section
